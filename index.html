Hi, I'm Kasra Banijamali :)

I am a Ph.D. researcher and engineer focused on developing automated quality-control and intelligent inspection systems that enable machines to monitor, evaluate, and improve processes with minimal human intervention. My work combines artificial intelligence, machine vision, sensing, robotics, and data-driven methods to develop practical systems for real-time quality assessment and process control.

My current research focuses on automated quality control in robotic 3D concrete printing, where I develop systems for in-process monitoring of geometry, material behavior, and mechanical performance. Beyond construction and 3D printing, my experience in advanced manufacturing and automotive engineering has allowed me to explore how these technologies can be adapted to different industrial environments.

Ultimately, my goal is to develop intelligent, scalable quality-control systems that can move from the laboratory into real-world applications across manufacturing, automotive, construction, and other automated production systems.
